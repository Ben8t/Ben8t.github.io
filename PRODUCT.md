# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Hiring managers, prospective collaborators, conference organizers, and a smaller secondary audience of AI Sessions bookers. They land here in evaluation mode — scanning credibility, recent work, and personality in under a minute. Most arrive from a referral, a conference, LinkedIn, or a search for the author's name.

The primary job-to-be-done is "decide quickly whether Benoit is the right person to talk to" — for a role, a collaboration, a speaking slot, or a paid session. The site needs to communicate craft and substance fast, not entertain.

## Product Purpose

A personal portfolio for Benoit, a product engineer working at the intersection of data infrastructure, developer tools, and thoughtful UX. The site is the canonical surface for his projects, experience, writing, and the paid AI Sessions offering.

Success looks like a hiring manager or collaborator reading two project pages, feeling confident about expertise, and reaching out. Secondary success is an AI Sessions booking.

## Brand Personality

Warm-personal, considered, expert. Three-word personality: **curious, precise, human**. The voice is first-person without being self-important; the tone is confident without being loud. It should evoke the feel of a thoughtful practitioner's working notebook — not a marketing landing page, not a SaaS product.

Tonal anchors: Robin Sloan's site, Maggie Appleton's garden, Anh Dang's portfolio. Editorial pacing, generous whitespace, deliberate typography, a single warm accent.

## Anti-references

- **SaaS landing-page template**: gradient hero + feature-card grid + big-CTA blocks + hero-metric stats. Avoid the entire shape.
- **Crypto/AI dashboard aesthetic**: neon-on-black, glassmorphism, animated gradient meshes, dark-blue-purple palettes.
- **Bootstrap/agency template feel**: identical 3-column card rows, stock iconography, generic "modern" decoration.
- **Overly decorative**: animated backgrounds, decorative emojis, glassmorphic surfaces, gratuitous motion.

## Design Principles

1. **Substance over surface.** The work itself is the asset. Decoration should never compete with project content.
2. **Editorial pacing.** Treat the page like a printed spread — generous rhythm, deliberate hierarchy, no wall-to-wall density.
3. **Warm restraint.** One accent system (silver for identity, gold reserved for the paid offer). Tinted neutrals, no flat grays, no #000/#fff.
4. **Practice what you preach.** As a product engineer who talks about craft, the site has to *be* crafted. Misalignments, drift, and inconsistencies undermine the message faster than any copy can rescue it.
5. **Respect the reader's time.** Hiring managers scan. Make the first 5 seconds count: name, role, recent work, one credible signal.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA target. Specific considerations:
- All gradient-text passes contrast against background in both themes (or has a solid fallback for screen readers / forced-colors mode).
- Theme toggle persists; respects `prefers-color-scheme` on first load.
- Respects `prefers-reduced-motion` for any future motion.
- Keyboard navigable; focus indicators visible against both light and dark backgrounds.
- All project thumbnails have descriptive alt text (already present).
